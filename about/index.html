Our Mission

Empower every Indian accounting practice and business with intelligent automation.

Empower every Indian accounting practice and business with intelligent automation that reduces manual effort, improves accuracy, and makes GST workflows effortless.

India’s economy is digitising at an extraordinary pace. Yet, in thousands of accounting firms and finance teams, critical work still depends on manual data entry, physical invoice handling, and repetitive reconciliation. This mismatch between ambition and process is the exact problem we exist to solve.

Docuro AI exists to close that gap — to give accountants tools that feel intelligent, fast, and trustworthy, so they can serve more clients, deliver real-time insights, and build the automated practice of the future.

The Problem We Solve

Manual accounting is still a structural bottleneck for modern Indian finance teams.

Every day, Chartered Accountants and tax professionals across India spend hours on tasks that should not be manual: reading invoice data, entering line items into Tally, computing GST, checking for duplicates, and generating filing-ready reports.

Repetitive manual data entry

Drains valuable professional time that should go toward judgment, advisory, and client service.

Human errors in tax details

Amounts, GSTINs, and tax calculations get harder to trust when teams are stretched thin.

Scattered workflows

Accountants juggle multiple tools, spreadsheets, emails, and physical documents to finish one process.

Slow reconciliation cycles

Delays filing and increases compliance risk for firms managing multiple entities or deadlines.

Lack of real-time visibility

Pending work, document status, and GST liability stay buried until too late in the month.

Linear headcount pressure

Growing teams often add people before they add infrastructure, which limits scalable margins.

Why Docuro AI Was Created

Accountants do not need to be replaced. They need to be liberated.

Docuro AI started with a simple, powerful realisation: accountants do not need to be replaced — they need to be liberated. The idea was born inside a technology team working closely with practicing CAs.

We saw first-hand how much cognitive effort and time went into tasks that machines could handle faster and more reliably. Existing tools were either generic OCR apps or heavy enterprise suites. We wanted something serious, simpler, and purpose-built for Indian accounting needs.

What if AI could understand Indian accounting work the way CAs actually operate?

That question became Docuro AI. Our vision was never to build just another accounting app. We set out to create a platform that understands GST logic, CA workflows, and the journey from raw invoice to Tally-ready voucher.

Built for adoption, not just ambition

The platform is designed to feel implementation-light while still carrying real AI capability, secure architecture, and operational depth.

Product Workflow

From document capture to export, the workflow is built to remove friction.

At its core, Docuro AI automates the accounting workflow from document capture to voucher generation and GST reports. Every piece is designed to reduce friction and give professionals a single, unified workspace.

01

AI-Powered Invoice Extraction

Upload a photo, PDF, or even a WhatsApp-forwarded bill. The engine reads vendor name, invoice number, date, taxable amount, and GST breakdown without manual templates.

02

GST Workflow Automation

The platform automatically calculates CGST, SGST, and IGST, prepares line items, and maps transactions correctly to save hours per client per month.

03

Auto-Ledger, Review, and Tally Export

Docuro AI handles ledger entries, duplicate detection, review queues, and one-click Tally export for consistent, reconciliation-ready output.
