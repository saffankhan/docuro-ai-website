App Overview

What Docuro AI Does

Docuro AI is an AI-powered accounting automation platform built for Indian Chartered Accountants, tax professionals, and SMBs. It converts invoices, bills, and receipts into ledger-ready data, automates GST workflows, and exports directly to Tally.

Security & Trust

How We Protect Customer Data

Encrypted Transport

All API traffic is served over HTTPS / TLS 1.2+ with HSTS enabled.

Data Isolation

Per-tenant isolation in Supabase with Row Level Security on every table.

PCI-DSS Payments

Card & UPI data is handled entirely by Razorpay — never stored on our servers.

Auth & Sessions

Mobile OTP & JWT-based sessions with short token lifetimes and refresh rotation.

Backups

Automated daily backups with point-in-time recovery on the primary database.

Audit Logs

Sensitive financial actions are logged for traceability and compliance.

Docuro AI uses Razorpay for all INR subscription billing. No card or UPI data ever touches our servers.
