            Frequently Asked Questions
            
Clear answers before you choose a plan.

          


          

            

              How does billing work?
              
You are billed monthly on your subscription date, and invoices can be managed from your account dashboard.

            

            

              Is there a free trial?
              
New firms can begin with a limited Pro trial experience before committing to a paid plan.

            

            

              Does Docuro AI support GST workflows?
              
Yes, plans are designed around invoice extraction, GST automation, and filing-oriented reporting.

            

            

              Is Tally integration included in all plans?
              
Tally export is available on Pro and Enterprise plans.

            

            

              Can I upgrade or downgrade later?
              
Yes, plans can be changed later based on your evolving invoice volume and feature needs.

            

            

              Can I cancel anytime?
              
Yes. Cancellation stops future billing while preserving access until the current billing period ends.
